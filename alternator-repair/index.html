Alternator Repair FAQs

What are signs of an alternator problem?

Common symptoms include a battery warning light, repeated dead batteries, dim or flickering lights, and electrical accessories acting strangely. Testing is needed to identify the cause.

Do I need a new battery or alternator?

Not necessarily. A weak battery, faulty alternator, poor connections or another electrical issue can cause similar symptoms. We recommend diagnosing the charging system before replacing parts.
